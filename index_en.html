A local reality with international reach

Smilebots

The Smilebots are a robotics team made up of high school and university students from the Lugano region. In recent years we have competed in the world championships in Dallas, St. Louis, and Taiwan, winning prestigious international awards.

Our association is non-profit. We recently moved our headquarters to Lamone, where our students design, build, program, and test robots year-round. This project thrives thanks to volunteer dedication and the vital support of sponsors who believe in youth education and technological innovation.
